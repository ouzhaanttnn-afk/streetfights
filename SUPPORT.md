## 🥊 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. How do I save my game progress?
Your game automatically saves after every battle, gym workout, gear change, and skill acquisition. Data is saved directly to your device storage so you can play offline anytime.

### 2. How can I transfer my save to another device?
1. On your current device, open the **Settings (⚙️)** menu from the top header.
2. Tap **"Export Save"** to copy your save code to the clipboard.
3. On your new device, open **Settings**, paste the code into the import box, and tap **"Import Save"**.

### 3. I found a bug or have a feature suggestion. Where do I report it?
We actively track all bug reports and community suggestions on our public GitHub repository:  
👉 **[Submit a Bug Report or Request](https://github.com/ouzhaanttnn-afk/streetfights/issues)**

### 4. How do I reset my game to start over from Stage 1-1?
Open the in-game **Settings (⚙️)**, scroll down to the bottom, and tap the crimson **"Reset Career"** button. Confirm the alert to reset all progress.
