App: Street Striker: Zero to Legend • Developer: Oğuzhan Alper Tosun • Platform: iOS

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How is my progress saved?
Your progress is automatically saved to your device storage whenever you fight, upgrade stats in the gym, or obtain loot.
2. How can I transfer my save?
Open the in-game Settings (⚙️) and tap Export Save. On your other device, tap Import Save and paste the code.
3. How do I report bugs or request features?
We actively review all player feedback and bug reports through our public GitHub Help Desk.
