## 2. Information We Collect and Process

### A. Local Gameplay & Progress Data
All game progression, player level, coins, gems, unlocked skills, equipped gear, and cosmetic settings are stored strictly locally on your device (via local app storage).
* **We do not transmit your game progress to any proprietary external database.**
* **We do not collect passwords, financial data, or sensitive personal identifiers.**

### B. Rewarded Advertising (Google AdMob)
The App may integrate Google AdMob to deliver optional rewarded video advertisements (e.g., in exchange for in-game revives or coin bonuses). When ads are shown, Google AdMob may collect and process:
* Device advertising identifiers (e.g., Apple IDFA, subject to your iOS App Tracking Transparency permission).
* IP address and general approximate geolocation.
* Anonymous ad interaction and performance metrics.

For additional information on Google's privacy practices, please consult [Google's Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

### C. Diagnostic & Crash Reports
Anonymous crash logs and performance telemetry may be collected automatically by Apple through TestFlight and iOS if you have enabled diagnostic sharing on your device.

---

## 3. How Information Is Used
Any collected information is utilized solely to:
* Ensure offline-capable gameplay and save progress.
* Provide player-requested bonus ads and associated rewards.
* Identify software bugs and optimize gameplay performance across iOS devices.

---

## 4. Third-Party Sharing
We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal information. Information is only processed through standard, certified SDKs (Apple App Store and Google AdMob) for functional app delivery.

---

## 5. Data Retention and Erasure
* Because gameplay saves reside locally on your device, you maintain full control over your data.
* You can permanently reset all progress by selecting **Settings > Reset Career** inside the App.
* Deleting or uninstalling the App from your device removes all stored local files and data immediately.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy
The App is designed for a general audience and does not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
